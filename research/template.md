## Evidence Labels

Use one label for every material claim:

- `Documented`: stated by an official source.
- `Sandbox-observed`: observed in a sanitized provider test environment.
- `Inferred`: reasoned from evidence but not explicitly guaranteed.
- `Unknown`: not established by available evidence.
